If you’re supporting someone else

You can help by listening without judgment, encouraging professional or peer support, and taking care of your own well-being. You do not have to fix everything. Learning about addiction and recovery can make conversations more constructive.

Al-Anon and similar family groups can be helpful for people affected by someone else’s drinking or drug use.
