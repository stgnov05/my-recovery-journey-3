Behind the Journey

Even after the storm, there is hope.

My name is Steven. For more than three decades, alcohol shaped almost every part of my life — my work, my relationships, my health, and the way I saw myself. I lost jobs I cared about, strained relationships that mattered, and spent years wondering whether a different kind of life was even possible.

I’m not writing this from a place of having it all figured out. I’m writing it from the middle of the work: choosing recovery one day at a time, rebuilding trust, strengthening my faith in God, and learning who I can become without alcohol.

“Your past is part of your story, but it does not have to be the end of your story.”
