My Recovery Journey

My past is part of my story. It does not have to be the end of it.

Steven Gregory · Founder

Why I’m beginning again

For 34 years, alcohol was woven into almost every part of my life. What started as something I thought I could control slowly became the thing that controlled me. Over time it cost me jobs I valued, damaged relationships that mattered, and left me with a version of myself I barely recognized.

There were seasons when I felt completely lost — angry about the years that had slipped away, uncertain whether I could ever rebuild stability, and unsure if peace or purpose were still possible for someone like me. I’m not sharing this for pity. I’m sharing it because I know other people have felt exactly that way.

Today I’m choosing recovery. I know that rebuilding a life takes time, effort, support, and honesty. I don’t have all the answers, and I won’t pretend that I do. Some days feel hopeful. Others feel heavy. Both are part of the process.

I don’t want addiction to write the rest of my story.

What the work looks like right now

Recovery for me is not a single dramatic moment. It’s a series of daily decisions: showing up to meetings, reaching out when isolation starts to sound appealing, practicing gratitude even when it feels forced, and asking God for strength on the days when my own runs out.

I’m learning to face the damage without drowning in it. I’m working to rebuild trust with people I hurt. I’m discovering that structure, rest, and honest conversation are not optional extras — they are part of how I stay free.

Faith in the middle of it

My Christian faith is not a polished success story. I’ve prayed through hope and through frustration. I’ve asked for strength, direction, peace, and a reason to look forward to tomorrow. One passage that stays with me is Joel 2:25 — God speaking of restoring the years that were lost. I cannot undo the past. I can make different choices today and trust that restoration is still possible.

“I cannot go back and reclaim the past. But I can begin building something different today.”

That’s why I started this website: to share what I’m learning, to be honest about the struggles, and to remind someone else that they don’t have to face recovery alone.

One day at a time. One step toward a new life.

What I’m working toward

🌱

Lasting recovery

Building healthy routines, seeking support, and making the next right choice even when it’s hard.

🤝

Connection & community

Growing meaningful friendships, finding accountability, and encouraging others on their journeys.

☀️

Faith & purpose

Trusting God as I rebuild, discovering new opportunities, and learning to live with hope again.

There’s still more to the story

Recovery isn’t about a perfect past or a perfect tomorrow. It’s about taking an honest step forward today.
