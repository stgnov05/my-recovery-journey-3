Faith for the Journey

Faith · Grace · Restoration

My Christian faith is part of my story of recovery. Here you’ll find Scripture that has mattered to me, a few reflections, and an open invitation: wherever you are in your beliefs, you’re welcome here.

Faith has not made the hard days disappear. It has given me somewhere to take the hard days — and a reason to keep choosing the next right step.

A passage that stays with me

Joel 2:25 (NLT)

“The Lord says, ‘I will give you back what you lost…’”

This verse reminds me of God’s promise of restoration and the hope of a new beginning. I cannot undo the years that addiction took. I can trust that restoration is still possible and that today is not the end of the story.

Faith in the middle of recovery

For me, faith looks less like constant confidence and more like returning — returning to prayer when I’m discouraged, returning to community when isolation feels easier, returning to the belief that my life still has purpose.

Some days prayer is simple: “Help.” Some days it is longer. Both count.

If faith is part of your recovery, you are not alone in that. If you are unsure about faith or come from a different tradition, you are still welcome to use the tools, read the story, and take what is useful.

Practices that help me

  • Short, honest prayer at the start or end of the day
  • Reading a small portion of Scripture without pressure to “get something profound”
  • Sitting with the grounding exercise when anxiety rises
  • Reaching out to a trusted person instead of isolating
  • Remembering that grace is for the imperfect process, not only the polished outcome
