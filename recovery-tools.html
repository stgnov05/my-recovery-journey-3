🧭 When a Moment Feels Difficult

Try this simple 5-4-3-2-1 grounding exercise to reconnect with the present.

  1. Name 5 things you can see.
  2. Notice 4 things you can feel (chair, clothes, air, feet on the floor).
  3. Listen for 3 things you can hear.
  4. Notice 2 things you can smell (or like the smell of).
  5. Notice 1 thing you can taste (or take a sip of water).

Then consider one small, safe next step: drink water, take a short walk, contact a supportive person, or attend a meeting.
